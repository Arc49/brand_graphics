# Third-party sounds

| Folder | Pack | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `kenney_interface/` (`ki_*`, 100 sounds) | Interface Sounds 1.0 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 (public domain) | https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds |
| `kenney_ui/` (`kui_*`, 51 sounds) | UI Audio | Kenney | CC0 1.0 (public domain) | https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio |

Both packs are free for commercial use and redistribution, with no attribution
required (crediting Kenney is appreciated). Each folder keeps the original
`LICENSE.txt`. Files were converted to 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV and loudness-normalised
to the UI tier with `sound/import_pack.py`.

The `desktop/` and `console/` folders are **original synthesised** sounds in the
style of desktop-OS alerts and handheld-console menus. Apple's and Nintendo's
own system sounds are copyrighted and are deliberately **not** included.
Published videos using them risk Content ID claims and takedowns.
